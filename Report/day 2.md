## 💡 Key Takeaways & Patterns
*   Recognized recursion as fundamentally building on the idea of breaking a problem into a smaller identical subproblem plus a base case — this became clearer while progressing from simple "print N times" to more applied problems like array reversal and Fibonacci.
*   Noticed that many "basic recursion" problems (print 1 to N, N to 1, sum, factorial) share a nearly identical structure, just with different operations performed before/after the recursive call — reinforcing the pattern of recursion vs. tail recursion.
*   Chose to attempt problems independently first before checking the optimal approach, which helped build confidence in constructing base recursive solutions from scratch.
